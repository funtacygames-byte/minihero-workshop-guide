## Run a trial

Ask a Chinese-speaking first-time mod author and an independent reader using the English guide to start at [the first lesson](../tutorials/first-mod.md). Provide the guide, game, and tools without orally filling in missing steps. With their consent, record actions and stopping points; do not collect account credentials.

Both readers should create Sources, build and load the 2→4 change, defeat the fixed-stage guard and win through the exit, receive the specific treasure, check next-stage shield, and diagnose a deliberately wrong entry or missing registration. They can then choose the character, enemy, or bilingual-asset lesson.
